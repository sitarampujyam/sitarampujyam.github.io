I’m Sitaram Pujyam, a BS (Research) student at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru. This is my personal website, where I’ll share my work, interests, and other cool things!

The reason I’m making this website is simple. Whenever I’m learning something new, especially something complicated, most sources explain it in an unnecessarily complicated way. After hours of reading from multiple sources and struggling with a topic, you realize it was actually quite simple. You find a way to make it click and think, “Why the hell didn’t they explain it this way?” That frustration is why this website exists.

As a complete beginner, I hope I can avoid the curse of knowledge. As I learn new things, instead of keeping notes to myself, I’ll put them here in case someone finds them useful someday. I’ll explain each topic in the most intuitive way I can, based on how I learned it. That could be insanely useful. Even with the best AI, sometimes an explanation just doesn’t click because it uses language that isn’t intuitive. Only a human can understand what another human might find unintuitive.
